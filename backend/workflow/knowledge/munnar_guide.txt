Destination: Munnar, Kerala, India

Overview:
Munnar is Kerala's premier hill station nestled in the Western Ghats, famous for sprawling tea plantations, mist-covered mountain peaks, cool weather, cascading waterfalls, and rich biodiversity.

Key Attractions:
- Eravikulam National Park (Rajamalai): Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goat and Anamudi, the highest peak in South India.
- Mattupetty Dam & Lake: Popular for speedboating, pedal boating, and scenic reservoir views surrounded by tea slopes.
- Echo Point & Kundala Lake: Scenic valley where voices echo; Kundala lake offers traditional Shikara boat rides.
- Top Station: Located at 1,700m on the Kerala-Tamil Nadu border offering sweeping panoramic views of the Western Ghats.
- KDHP Tea Museum: Historic museum showcasing tea manufacturing history from the 1880s, machinery, and tea tasting.
- Attukad Waterfalls: Beautiful multi-tiered waterfall set amidst rolling hills, ideal for photography.
- Pothamedu Viewpoint: Stunning sunset viewpoint offering vistas of tea, coffee, and cardamom plantations.
- Blossom International Park: Landscaped gardens with flower beds, cycling trails, and walking paths.

Curated Activities & Experiences:
- Guided tea plantation walking tour and factory tea tasting.
- Speedboat safari at Mattupetty reservoir.
- Scenic drive along the Gap Road with mountain cliff views.
- Sampling local delicacies: Authentic Kerala Appam with vegetable stew, Malabar snacks, and freshly made chocolates.
- Photography at panoramic viewpoints during early morning mist.

Travel & Route Advice:
- Route 1 (Mattupetty Circuit): Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Kundala Dam, and Top Station are along the same road and best visited together.
- Route 2 (National Park Circuit): Eravikulam National Park and Tea Museum are on the northern route.
- Mornings are best for wildlife sightings at Eravikulam; early booking is recommended.
