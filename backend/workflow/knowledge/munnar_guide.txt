
Destination: Munnar, Kerala, India

Munnar is a hill station known for tea plantations, mountain scenery, cool climate, and nature tourism.

Popular attractions:
- Eravikulam National Park
- Mattupetty Dam
- Echo Point
- Top Station
- Tea Museum
- Kundala Lake
- Tea plantations

Activities:
- Tea plantation visits
- Sightseeing
- Boating
- Nature walks
- Photography

Travel information:
Munnar is suitable for couples, families, and nature lovers.
Travelers should consider travel time between attractions while planning their itinerary.
Weather conditions may affect outdoor activities.

The itinerary should avoid scheduling too many distant attractions on the same day.
