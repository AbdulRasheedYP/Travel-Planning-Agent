Destination: Wayanad, Kerala, India

Overview:
Wayanad is a tranquil highland district in Kerala set among the Nilgiri Biosphere, renowned for misty mountains, dense spice and coffee plantations, prehistoric caves, waterfalls, and rich tribal heritage.

Key Attractions:
- Banasura Sagar Dam: Asia's second largest earthen dam, surrounded by misty hill islands, offering speedboat rides and zipline adventures.
- Edakkal Caves: Natural rock shelters featuring Neolithic petroglyphs and 6,000-year-old stone age carvings, reached via a short mountain climb.
- Chembra Peak & Heart-Shaped Lake (Hridaya Saras): The highest peak in Wayanad, famous for scenic trekking trails and a natural perennial heart-shaped lake.
- Soochipara Falls (Sentinel Rock Waterfall): Dramatic three-tiered waterfall cascading into a large pool surrounded by cliff faces and deciduous forest.
- Pookode Lake: Picturesque natural freshwater lake surrounded by evergreen forests, famous for lotus flowers, pedal boating, and walking trails.
- En Uru Tribal Heritage Village: Traditional tribal village in Vythiri showcasing heritage huts, indigenous art, and tribal lifestyle.
- Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary (Muthanga): Dense forest sanctuary home to wild Asian elephants, spotted deer, bison, and diverse bird species.
- Kuruva Island (Kuruva Dweep): 950-acre protected river island delta on the Kabini River, known for bamboo rafting and river walks.
- Lakkidi View Point & Chain Tree: Gateway viewpoint to Wayanad with sweeping mountain valley panoramas.

Curated Activities & Experiences:
- Bamboo rafting along the peaceful streams of the Kabini River.
- Adventure speedboating and zipline across the Banasura reservoir.
- Trekking through tea and cardamom estates around Meppadi.
- Morning open-jeep wildlife safari through Muthanga forest.
- Tasting authentic local Wayanad specialties: Bamboo rice payasam, Wayanad spice-infused teas, and local Malabar cuisine.

Travel & Route Advice:
- South Wayanad Circuit: Vythiri, Lakkidi, Pookode Lake, En Uru, Meppadi, and Soochipara Falls are close together and best visited on the same day.
- North & East Circuit: Banasura Sagar Dam, Edakkal Caves, and Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary should be scheduled together on another day to minimize road transit.
- Chembra Peak trek permits are issued early morning by the forest office in limited numbers per day.
